# Why the programme starts with a database

Most coordinator questions are joins between participants, relationships, sessions and goals. Ask for the overdue pairs and their unfinished development work, then record the next decision. The records can be owned and changed without building a participant portal first.

The free version produces private, read-only HTML programme reviews and relationship agendas. It does not provide participant self-service, mobile notifications, chat, calendar booking, shared editing, offline work or group mentoring. Enterprise DNA can build the views and connections needed around the same database. The base has no delivery or notification service.

One coordinator or a trusted operations team runs it through Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Cursor. Participant agreement and feedback are collected through your existing process. A shared deployment needs its access controls and recovery tested before use.
